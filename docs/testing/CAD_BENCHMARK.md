# Conversational CAD Benchmark

The benchmark stores prompts, input documents, expected dimensions, required topology, editability checks, exports, reopen checks, retries, elapsed time, provider usage, and failure diagnostics.

The provider suites use the `vibecad-benchmark-case-attempt-v2` evidence contract. One record represents one case in one attempt. Each record identifies the provider, model, executor, case, and attempt. It contains results for geometry, dimensions, constraints, editability, follow-up work, reopen, and export. It also contains question counts, retry counts, normalized usage, elapsed time, artifacts, and failure diagnostics. The series score uses the total number of case attempts as its denominator. It fails closed when a case is missing or when required evidence is incomplete.

The contract separates deterministic runs from live-model runs. A deterministic run records human instruction adherence as `not_rated` with a reason. A live-model run requires a bounded human rating, a reviewer identifier, and review notes. Therefore, a deterministic score cannot satisfy a live conversational target.

Tier 1 covers exact primitives and edits. Its target is at least 95 percent valid completion. Tier 2 covers functional parts and has an 85 percent target without manual CAD work. Tier 3 covers assemblies, drawing packages, imported STEP edits, CNC parts, interference repair, and parameter variants.

The repository now has a versioned seven-case Tier 1 deterministic CAD capability baseline. Run `tools/run_cad_benchmark.py`. It creates real FCStd artifacts, validates geometry, saves, closes, reopens, recomputes, and validates again. The export case also checks its STL artifact. Results use schema `vibecad-cad-benchmark-result-v1` and remain under `build/benchmark/tier1`.

The 2026-07-22 Apple Silicon baseline passed 7 of 7 cases. This is infrastructure evidence only. Its executor is labeled `deterministic-offline-capability-baseline`. It does not measure provider intent extraction, tool selection, retries, question count, or instruction adherence. Therefore, it does not prove the 95 percent conversational Tier 1 product target. A provider-driven runner and repeated-trial score remain required before that target can pass.

The repository also has a transactional provider baseline. Run `tools/run_provider_cad_benchmark.py --trials 3`. Each trial drives the public provider tool surface in the FreeCAD GUI lifecycle. The first workflow creates native editable geometry, uses selected-face language for a centered through-hole, and uses selected-feature language for a fillet. A separate workflow creates a 60 by 40 by 25 mm open-top enclosure with exact 2 mm walls through native sketch, pad, and thickness features. The provider must update each design brief, pass candidate validation, promote four accepted revisions, save, close, reopen, recompute, and compare exact dimensions, volume, and shape validity. The series uses schema `vibecad-benchmark-series-v2` and writes its results under `build/benchmark/tier1-provider`.

The 2026-07-22 Apple Silicon transactional baseline passed 3 of 3 series trials. Each result contains successful selected-hole, fillet, dimension-change, hollow-enclosure, STL-export, and reopen checks. The dimension edit changes the original sketch constraint and proves that downstream features recompute. The export uses the typed policy and RBAC boundary and produces a content-bound project artifact without a false CAD revision. Its executor is `deterministic-provider-transactional-baseline`, and `live_model_score` is false. This evidence validates the provider-to-CAD transaction path, explicit selection path, shell workflow, dependency-aware edit, and export boundary. It does not measure live-model intent extraction or tool choice, so it does not satisfy the 95 percent conversational Tier 1 target.

The baseline now covers all seven Tier 1 prompt classes: exact box, centered hole, rounded edges, hollow enclosure, dimension change, mirrored feature, and STL export. Each of the three repeated series trials passed all class checks with 42 typed tool calls, six accepted revisions, three reopened FCStd documents, and one verified STL artifact. This is a complete deterministic-provider transaction baseline, not a live-model score.

The Tier 2 transactional suite has ten functional-part cases. Run `tools/run_tier2_provider_benchmark.py --trials 3`. The wall-bracket case creates one fully editable native L-profile, a 30 mm pad, and two 4 mm through mounting holes. The motor-adapter case creates an 80 mm disc with a 20 mm shaft bore and four equal 5 mm holes on a 60 mm pitch circle. The battery-tray case creates a 100 by 60 by 20 mm open shell with 2.5 mm walls and four symmetric bottom mounts. The camera-mount case creates a plate with a center clearance hole and two constrained adjustment slots. The pipe-clamp case creates an annular clamp with a radial split and two through mounts. The ventilated-cover case creates five constrained through-slots in a thin plate. The electronics-enclosure case creates two separate editable bodies: an open housing and a removable M3 lid.

The flanged-coupling case creates one 80 mm flange and one 40 mm hub. It has a 20 mm through-bore and four 6 mm holes on a 60 mm pitch circle. The simple-hinge case creates four separate editable bodies. It has two 40 by 30 by 3 mm leaves, one 8 mm barrel with a 6 mm bore, and one 5 mm pin. The bolt-pattern-plate case creates one 100 by 80 by 8 mm plate. It has a 20 mm center bore and eight 6 mm holes on a 60 mm pitch circle.

Each Tier 2 case uses typed provider tools and native parametric features. Each case must have one accepted revision. The suite checks exact feature types, solid counts, cylindrical-hole topology where applicable, analytic volume, and required dimensions. It saves, closes, reopens, recomputes, and checks the result again. All 26 sketches in the current suite must have zero degrees of freedom and no conflicting constraint.

The 2026-07-22 Apple Silicon ten-case expansion passed its required fresh round. It passed 10 of 10 case attempts with 187 typed calls, ten accepted revisions, and ten reopened documents. The three new cases each passed exact volume, topology, editability, revision, and reopen checks. The live pre-save circular bound can differ from the exact constrained diameter by up to 0.016 mm. Therefore, a transient circular bound can use a 0.02 mm tolerance. The reopened dimensions, analytic volume, and topology must still pass. The clamp Y bound uses the analytic result after its split removes the outer circle's top point.

The executor is deterministic and `live_model_score` is false. This run does not measure live-model intent extraction or tool selection. It does not satisfy the 85 percent conversational Tier 2 target.
